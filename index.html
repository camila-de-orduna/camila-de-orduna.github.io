Camila De Orduna-Ortiz

I am an emerging Mexican-Canadian artist that focuses on portraiture and anatomy, drawing inspiration from the surrealist movement. After spending four years in a visual arts program in high school, I illustrated several novels, including L’Étrange Noël Fantôme by Canadian author Karine Glorieux (Bagnole Publishing, 2022) and the covers of Seda Araña, Otumba, and Las Torres de Arena (Pre-Textos Publishing, 2023) by Mexican-Canadian author Antolina Ortiz. I have attended live drawing sessions at the Westmount Art Centre and UQAM university. In addition to my passion for visual arts, I play classical violin since the age of four and currently form part of the Montreal Suzuki String Orchestra. I am about to begin my studies at McGill university in Physics.
